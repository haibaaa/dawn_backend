# Planit

University life is overwhelming, study materials are scattered, and it is hard to know what to prioritize. This Academic Planner centralizes your student experience into a high-performance, AI-powered hub designed to turn disorganized stress into a clear path to excellence.

## Key Features

* **Smart Task Prioritization**: Stop guessing what to work on. Our algorithm evaluates urgency and grade impact to tell you exactly what needs your attention first.
* **Grade Performance Portal**: Track your scores in real-time. The system calculates exactly what you need in your final exams to hit your target GPA.
* **AI Study Aids**: Upload lecture slides to automatically generate flashcards and explanatory content to make learning faster.
* **Global Resource Bank**: Access a shared library of past year papers and notes. Use our AI to turn these resources into tailored practice quizzes.

## Tech Stack

- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python)
- **Environment & Package Management**: uv
- **Database & Auth**: Supabase
- **Documentation**: OpenAPI / Swagger
